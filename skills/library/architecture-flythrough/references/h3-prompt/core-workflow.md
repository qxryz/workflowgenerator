# Core Workflow: Architectural Prompts

Read for every H3 prompt in this Skill. Follow [the package entry](../h3-prompt.md).

## Diagnose the hard part

- Identity: landmarks must retain distinguishing silhouettes, proportions, components, and independent identities.
- Space: preserve real building locations, scale relationships, entrances, indoor/outdoor connections, and occlusion.
- Motion: respect the approved camera route, speed changes, lens, and continuous timing.
- Style: preserve one Bible across views while allowing physically plausible screen-space lighting changes.
- Source preservation: retain RGB geometry and motion without forcing its placeholder appearance onto the final image.
- Text/audio: assess exact signage or sound requirements only when requested; do not add them as standard packaging.

## Construct the visual brief

State the location and purpose; describe colors, materials, light, atmosphere, and texture concretely; lock each subject; map continuous movement to the manifest; finish with targeted constraints against likely failures. Use exact text spelling only when text is required.

## Defaults from this workflow

Use the established 10–15-second duration, 16:9 composition, realistic cinematic appearance unless changed by the user, restrained location ambience, and no unsolicited background music. Finish at the approved architectural landing composition. Do not add branding or a separate end card.

## Self-check

Does the prompt preserve the approved intent, geometry, route, and style? Are all 3–4 image roles and times explicit? Is the ending the same camera destination? Are phases continuous within one shot? Are multiple landmarks protected against swapping or merging? Are source-preservation and appearance-change clauses compatible? Are sound and optional text faithful to the request? Are the negative constraints specific rather than a generic list?
