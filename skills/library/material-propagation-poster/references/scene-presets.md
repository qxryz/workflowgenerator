# Scene Presets

Choose one primary preset and, if useful, one modifier. Keep the subject plate and layout rules from the primary preset.

| Preset | Texture mapping | Growth mode | Camera and pacing | Main guardrails | Good outputs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Portrait / sculpture | Face, hair, neck, and garment masks; follow planes and folds | Branching flow or bloom | Locked portrait; seed at temple, eye-cover, collar, or chest; 5s with 0.6s hold | Preserve eyes, nose, lips, silhouette, sculptural highlights, and skin/stone volume | Cover loop, gallery screen, 4:5 social |
| Fashion / product | Seams, panels, edges, and material zones | Wrap and cascade or contour fill | Locked 3/4 or macro; slow 4-6s reveal; no invented accessories | Preserve logo, stitching, hardware, product proportions, and reflections | Product reveal, PDP hero, launch post |
| Architecture / interior | Facades, windows, grids, stair lines, or room planes | Contour fill or branching flow | Locked wide shot or 2% push-in; measured route across planes | Keep perspective, vanishing lines, windows, and structural edges stable | Event poster, venue screen, architectural reel |
| Editorial cover / poster | Subject mask inside a locked layout plate | Bloom or contour fill | Locked frame; 0.8s clean opening and 0.8s clean hold | Do not redraw type, logos, title, or margins; composite type afterward when needed | Magazine cover, campaign grid, 9:16 story |
| Nature / organic | Veins, petals, bark, water, or terrain zones | Branching flow or bloom | Locked macro or gentle 3% drift; organic easing | Preserve species cues and overall silhouette; avoid random extra flora | Botanical loop, album cover, installation |
| Data / tech / abstract | Grid, nodes, waveform, heatmap, or selected vector regions | Contour fill or branching signal | Locked screen-like composition; 3-5s with crisp settle | Keep grid alignment, labels, and signal logic; no visual clutter | UI backdrop, data story, tech campaign |

## Modifiers

- **Thermal-spark**: map a cool-to-hot palette with a small number of bright star-shaped peaks; use sparse peaks and keep the gradient continuous.
- **Botanical-ink**: grow fine stems first, then leaves/flowers; preserve line quality and use a soft stop at the mask boundary.
- **Halftone-pop**: reveal dot scale from coarse to fine; keep print-registration and avoid photographic noise.
- **Liquid-chrome**: flow along highlights and edges; preserve reflections and prevent melting geometry.
- **Fabric-weave**: expand along seams or grain direction; keep the weave scale stable and tactile.
- **Signal-glitch**: use short controlled scanline or waveform pulses; limit distortion to the chosen mask and return to a clean plate.
