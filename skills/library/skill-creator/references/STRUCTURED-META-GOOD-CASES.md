# Structured Market Metadata Good Cases

Use this file when authoring the new `structured-info` object. It is editorial guidance, not a
machine-validation rule; field limits come only from `spec/metadata.yml`.

## Writing principles

- `summary` states the core capability and value in one sentence.
- `best-for` uses 2–3 short labels describing content type, task, or style.
- `how-to-use` says what the user provides and the most important starting action.
- `outputs` lists final deliverables only; it does not describe execution steps.
- Fill Chinese and English independently while mirroring facts and information density; do not
  automatically copy legacy `summary-*` or `desc-*` fields.

## Good Cases

### 3D Animation Short Generator

**Summary:** Turn a story idea into a unified, character-consistent 3D animated short and deliver the finished film.

**Best For:** Stylized 3D｜Story Shorts｜Character Animation

**How to Use:** Provide a script or story idea, then choose duration, framing, and visual style to begin.

**Outputs:** Character and scene designs, shot list, storyboard, and finished 3D animation video.

### Minimalist Product Ad Generator

**Summary:** Turn product images and advertising requirements into a premium minimalist commercial that preserves product identity.

**Best For:** E-commerce Products｜Product Launches｜Minimalist Ads

**How to Use:** Upload product images, then choose the look, duration, framing, template, and copy approach.

**Outputs:** Selling-point copy, product anchor images, beat-synced storyboard, and finished ad.

### Brand Promo Video Generator

**Summary:** Turn verified brand assets and promotion goals into a clear, credible, presentation-ready brand video.

**Best For:** Brand Launches｜Website Promotion｜App Promotion

**How to Use:** Provide authorized brand assets and official materials, then confirm duration, audience, and focus.

**Outputs:** Brand fact sheet, creative storyboard, audio-visual assets, and finished promo video.

### H3 Prompt Expert

**Summary:** Organize video ideas, reference assets, and keyframes into controllable, directly usable H3 prompts.

**Best For:** Multimodal Prompts｜Image References｜Text UI Control

**How to Use:** Provide the video idea and references, select a task type, and fill in missing details.

**Outputs:** H3-formatted prompts, asset mapping, motion and sound directions, and text/UI constraints.

### Music Video Subtitle Generator

**Summary:** Turn music and lyrics into a rhythm-synced stylized MV with spatial subtitles and deliver the finished piece.

**Best For:** Lyric MVs｜Beat Sync｜Music Visuals

**How to Use:** Provide music, lyrics, and references, then confirm duration, music window, and framing.

**Outputs:** MV concept, beat timeline, shot prompts, and lyric typography plan.

### Paper Collage Explainer Generator

**Summary:** Turn copy or abstract ideas into a tactile paper-collage explainer animation with designed sound effects.

**Best For:** Explainers｜Opinion Content｜Social Shorts

**How to Use:** Provide copy or a topic, then choose framing, duration, palette, and audio needs.

**Outputs:** Visual metaphors, paper-collage stills, stop-motion animation, and collage sound effects.

### Micro-expression Video Generator

**Summary:** Turn a character image, script, or emotion phrase into subtle, believable, executable micro-expression direction.

**Best For:** Subtle Acting｜Emotional Turns｜Understated Dialogue

**How to Use:** Provide a character image or script, then specify shot duration and performance intensity.

**Outputs:** Timed acting prompts, movement details, continuity constraints, and performance clips.

### Handdrawn Live Video Generator

**Summary:** Fuse a live-action scene with rough glowing hand-drawn animation into a creative 15-second short.

**Best For:** Live-action Drawing｜Continuous Morphing｜Lifestyle Shorts

**How to Use:** Provide a live-action scene and contact action, then confirm mood, style, and framing.

**Outputs:** Hand-drawn fusion prompts, morphing camera rhythm, and an optional 15-second video.
